Why {{ pair.left.eventsWithinRadius | number }} against {{ pair.right.eventsWithinRadius | number }} is not a fair comparison. The catalogue holds effectively nothing below M4.0 anywhere in the archipelago, and detection improved 285-fold across the last century. Two places watched by different numbers of instruments produce different totals from the same seismicity. Read the M{{ comparableMagnitude.toFixed(1) }}+ row.

Assigned depths. 43% of the archive carries a depth that was never measured — 33 km, 10 km, 35 km and 15 km are conventions used when depth could not be resolved. They are marked with a dashed ring on the maps and counted separately above.

Storms are track proximity, not impact. A cyclone's damaging winds and rain reach far beyond the positions its analysts recorded, so both columns undercount exposure. Peak winds from different agencies sit on different averaging periods and do not convert into one another.

An unmapped fault is not an absent one. Traces come from the GEM compilation, which maps principal named faults at regional scale. The 2013 Bohol earthquake came from a fault no map showed until afterwards.
