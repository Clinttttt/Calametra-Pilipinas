  • Depth assigned, not measured. A fixed 33, 10, 35 or 15 km standing in for a value the agency could not resolve. It marks that an earthquake occurred, not how deep it was — and the share is far higher in the historical record, where the 33 km convention dominates.
  • Dominant scale. The scale most readings in the decade were measured on. Body-wave and moment magnitudes are different quantities, so a magnitude from one decade and a magnitude from another are frequently not the same kind of number.
  • Strongest. The largest value in the decade, shown with its scale and deliberately not ranked against other decades for the same reason.
